See the work Why recycled wood

Old wood, new purpose

We build with recycled wood. It keeps good lumber out of the landfill, and the nail holes, weathering and tight old grain give each piece a character new boards can't match.

  1. Rescued

    We collect wood that has already served one purpose and is ready for another.

  2. Cleaned and milled

    Each board is de-nailed, cleaned, planed and sorted by grain and colour.

  3. Built by hand

    We cut, join, sand and finish every piece ourselves, so no two are the same.
